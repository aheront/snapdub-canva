## Указания по проверке (testing-instructions)
About your app (400):
SnapDub (snapdub.ai) turns long videos, streams and podcasts into short vertical clips with captions. This app brings a person's finished SnapDub clips into their Canva design. It creates nothing inside Canva: the clips are made in the person's SnapDub account, and the app lists them and adds the chosen one to the page.

Testing steps (1000, no credentials here):
1. Open the SnapDub app and press Sign in with SnapDub.
2. In the window that opens, sign in with the test account from Log in details, then press Authorize.
3. The panel lists the account's videos, newest first. The test account has two finished videos. Older videos load as you scroll.
4. Pick a video to see its clips, best first. Click a clip to add it to the page, or drag it onto the page. A spinner shows on the clip while it uploads.
5. Empty state: an account with no videos shows No videos yet and a button that opens snapdub.ai. Error state: if SnapDub cannot be reached, the panel shows a message with Try again.
6. Sign out is at the bottom of the panel. New users can create a free SnapDub account from the same sign-in window.
